About

I am a Van Vleck Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. My postdoctoral mentor is Gheorghe Craciun. I also collaborate with Hanbaek Lyu, Qin Li, and Matthew Colbrook on Koopman theory, and with Wolfram Liebermeister on structural sensitivity analysis and metabolic control analysis.

I received my Ph.D. from the Department of Mathematical Sciences at KAIST, under the supervision of Jae Kyoung Kim. My research focuses primarily on mathematical biology and dynamical systems.
